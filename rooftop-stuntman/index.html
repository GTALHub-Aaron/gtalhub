How to play

  • The cannon sweeps up and down on its own. Hit LAUNCH! at the right moment.
  • In the air, hold FLIP! to spin, or hold Hero, Eagle or Heels to strike a pose.
  • Hold a pose and FLIP together to spin in that pose for a combo bonus. Let go before you land!
  • Bounce on trampolines, fly through drone rings, and dodge birds, blimps and saucers.
  • Fame unlocks stuntmen, stats unlock cannon skins, and there's a new Daily Challenge every day.
  • Keys: Space launch / flip, 123 tricks.
