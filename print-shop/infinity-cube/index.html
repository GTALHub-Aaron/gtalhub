Printing tips
  • Print flat as exported. No supports. Four links sit on the bed, two on top.
  • Clearance 0.35 mm suits most tuned printers at 0.2 mm layers. Raise it if the hinges fuse.
  • Keep "Hinge size" at 2.6 mm or more so the links stay strong.
  • After printing, flex each hinge gently before folding the whole cube.
  • Fold cycle: open the cube into the flat strip, fold its ends back to make a new cube, and repeat.
