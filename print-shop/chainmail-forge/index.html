Bigger gaps give a floppier fabric. The defaults are set for a 0.4 mm nozzle at 0.2 mm layers. Bar thickness and layer gap move in whole layers.

Sheet

Printing it

  • No supports. The high bars are short bridges between posts.
  • If links fuse at the bed, turn on elephant-foot compensation (about 0.1–0.15 mm) or raise the side gap.
  • Print bridges slowly with full fan. A brim is fine but skip a raft.
  • Flex it gently along both axes after printing to break any whiskers.
