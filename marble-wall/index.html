Marble Wall

Build: tap a part in the tray to stick it on the wall. Tap a part to select it, then drag to move it (dragging an unselected part just pans the view), twist the yellow knob (or ⟲ ⟳) to rotate. Track ends snap together.

Drops: marbles fall from the dropper on their own. Tap the timer to pause while you build. They score for distance rolled and for tricks: big air, transfers between parts, bumpers, bells, rings, loops and portals.

Combo: every trick raises the run's multiplier. A Goal Cups pay ×2.

Walls: fill a wall's milestone (marbles in Goal Cups, points earned, and uses of its glowing key item) to unlock the next wall. Hit loose items hard enough and they fall off the wall. Walls you leave keep dropping and earning.

Sandbox: unlimited free parts to build anything (no points). Find it in the menu or the Levels tab.

Challenges: fixed puzzles in the Levels tab. Land 5 marbles in the checkered Finish with limited parts. Use fewer parts for more stars and gems.

Grow: marbles auto-drop on every level; upgrade Drop Speed to drop faster. Spend points on parts, fancier marbles and boosts.

Rebuild: after clearing level 2, rebuild the wall from scratch for Gems. Gems buy permanent perks, and each unspent gem adds 2% to all points. Drag empty wall to pan; pinch or scroll to zoom.
