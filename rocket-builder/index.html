Challenges

For grown-ups

Tap pieces to build. Body tanks stack up (up to 5); tap a tank on the rocket to take it off. The orange push bar must be longer than the gray heavy bar or the rocket stays on the pad. Pointy noses cut through the air, fins keep it flying straight, and side boosters drop off when empty.

Every flight earns coins, more for each landmark passed (treetops 5 up to faraway stars 400, plus 10 just for flying). Pieces cost coins to unlock once, then they can be used as often as you like. A piece with a gold tag can be bought now; a gray tag means save up more. Progress and coins are saved on this device.

The trophy button opens 14 challenges, like reaching the clouds with no fins or stopping at the balloon without passing the clouds. Each pays bonus coins the first time; the next few unlock as earlier ones are done.
